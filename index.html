ABOUT DONGJI

About Guangdong Dongji Intelligent Equipment Co., Ltd.

Founded in 2011 and rooted in the Greater Bay Area, Dongji is a smart-equipment manufacturer with in-house R&D and design capabilities. With a 30,000㎡ base and fully-imported laser cutting, CNC punching, bending, welding, coating and assembly lines, we provide OEM / ODM / JDM smart cooking robot contract manufacturing for foodservice brands, equipment makers and distributors.

Backed by 15 years of manufacturing and 100+ patents, Dongji products ship to 50+ countries and regions including Australia, the US, Germany, Italy and Southeast Asia, and serve as the manufacturing partner for multiple international brands. We believe: leave manufacturing to the pros, so brands can focus on the market.

  • ✔ One-stop from sheet metal to finished unit — no multiple suppliers
  • ✔ Flexible MOQ + free rapid prototyping
  • ✔ English technical communication with localized docs and certification support
