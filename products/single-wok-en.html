Q & A

Q & A

Common questions about this model.

When should we consider the Single-Wok model?

Compact kitchens, delivery kitchens, quick-service restaurants, and businesses testing automation for the first time.

What staff work can it reduce?

It controls heat, timing, stirring and seasoning. Staff still prep and load, but don't manage every step.

How to plan peak-hour output?

Each batch holds up to 2.5 kg. Output depends on dish and prep; we recommend testing the real menu first.

Is day-to-day operation complex?

Staff load prepared ingredients, choose a dish and start. The machine follows the saved program.
