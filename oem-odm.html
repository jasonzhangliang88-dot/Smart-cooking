FREQUENTLY ASKED

OEM / ODM / JDM FAQ

Common questions from brands, distributors and foodservice buyers evaluating a cooking robot manufacturer.

Is Dongji a real smart cooking robot source factory?

Yes. Dongji owns a 30,000㎡ manufacturing base with in-house R&D, sheet-metal fabrication and assembly, and provides OEM / ODM / JDM contract manufacturing for smart cooking robots and commercial cooking automation.

Can you build to our design (OEM)?

Yes. Send us your drawings or sample and we manufacture to spec, including sheet-metal, electronics integration and final assembly.

Do you support private-label and custom models (ODM / JDM)?

Yes. We can brand our mature platforms or co-develop an exclusive model with you, including exterior, structure, control software and recipe system.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ is project-based. Dongji supports pilot runs and small batches as well as mass production, with free rapid prototyping to de-risk the start.

Which markets can you ship to?

Dongji products ship to 50+ countries and regions including the US, Germany, Italy, Australia and Southeast Asia, with certifications for major markets.
